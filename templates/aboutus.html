Who We Are

We are Cheek Middle School's proud National Junior Honor Society team. We volunteer to help the community since it's our duty, and we strive to do so, to make an impact. We want to help our community out with any volunteering jobs they have and want to spread love. We have over 50 students who would love to help everyone out and volunteer to make an impact in the community. If you have any volunteering opportunities in the Denton area for us, please contact the chapter advisor, Mrs. Fields at "number".

Why We Do What We Do

It sounds pretty simple, and it indeed is very simple why. We want to make an impact, create a change, and bring love wherever it is needed. Examples include the suicide prevention walk, sending letters to Mrs.Cheek, donating toys for Christmas, and the salvation army bell ringing. We also do a lot of fun activites here and then to keep ourselves entertained and the enjoyment going. We love what we do!
